Nº003 / Methodology

Product design methodology: four disciplines from research to validation.

Nº001

The Foundation

(Research & Empathy) : Every great product starts with understanding the user. I talk to users, observe real-world workflows, and study core problems. I focus on what people are trying to achieve and what friction is holding them back. Without deep empathy, design lacks substance and purpose.

Nº002

The Journey

(Strategy & Information Architecture) : Before designing screens, I plan the full product journey. I map how users move from one task to another, making sure everything feels clear and connected. Like navigating intuitive spaces, each step leads naturally to the next.

Nº003

The Craft

(Wireframing, Systems & Visual Design) : I build wireframes, interactive prototypes, and scalable design systems to explore layout, micro-interactions, and hierarchy. Once the structure works, I bring it to life with typography, motion, and visual clarity.

Nº004

Validation

(User Testing & Iteration) : I test interactive prototypes with real users to identify cognitive load and friction points. Then I refine and iterate. Through continuous feedback loops, I polish the experience until everything feels intuitive and effortless.
