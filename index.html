Nº003 / Methodology

Game-inspired design methodology — four disciplines from lore to playtest.

Nº001

The Lore

(Research & Empathy) — Every great game starts with understanding the player. I talk to users, observe their behavior, and study their needs. I focus on what they're trying to achieve and what's holding them back. If I don't understand the player, I can't design a meaningful experience.

Nº002

The Journey

(Strategy & Information Architecture) — Before designing screens, I plan the full journey. I map how users move from one step to another, making sure everything feels clear and connected. Like designing game levels, each step should lead naturally to the next.

Nº003

The Experience

(Wireframing, Prototyping & Visual Design) — I create wireframes and prototypes to explore layout, interactions, and flow. Once the structure works, I bring it to life with visuals, typography, and motion. This ensures the experience is both functional and engaging.

Nº004

Playtest

(User Testing & Iteration) — I test the design with real users to see where they struggle or get confused. Then I refine and improve the experience. Like playtesting a game, I keep improving it until everything feels smooth and satisfying.
