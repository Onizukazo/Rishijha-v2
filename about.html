The Designer

I am Rishi Jha, an independent Product & Experience Designer collaborating with ambitious startups, product teams, and forward-thinking enterprises. I bridge user research, complex systems architecture, and visual craft to design intuitive digital products, scalable design systems, and high-impact web and mobile applications—shipping work that moves at speed with zero unnecessary ceremony.

001 General Questions

  1. Nº001

    What services do you specialize in?

    +

    End-to-end Product Design (UI/UX), design systems architecture, and thoughtful interaction design. From initial discovery and user journey mapping to high-fidelity Figma components, interactive prototypes, and developer handoff, I build intuitive experiences for web apps, mobile apps, and SaaS platforms.

  2. Nº002

    Who do you work with?

    +

    Fast-growing venture-backed startups needing to ship a high-quality MVP, enterprise teams tackling complex workflow overhauls (such as financial platforms and government portals), and founders looking for a design partner who understands product strategy and technical feasibility.

  3. Nº003

    What organizations and brands have you worked with?

    +

    I have collaborated on high-impact digital products across fintech, government, enterprise, and consumer tech—including projects for Moneycontrol, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Samsung, the Unique Identification Authority of India (Aadhaar), Maharashtra Medical Council, and Leaf.

  4. Nº004

    What is your typical turnaround time?

    +

    Sprint deliverables, user flows, and interface iterations are typically delivered within 48 to 72 hours. Larger product initiatives—such as 0-to-1 web applications or comprehensive design system overhauls—are broken into structured weekly milestones with continuous visibility.

  5. Nº005

    How does your collaboration process work?

    +

    We start with an onboarding alignment call to review your product goals, technical constraints, and priorities. Work is tracked transparently in Notion or Linear with interactive Figma files. I provide regular asynchronous progress updates with Loom video walkthroughs explaining design rationale, keeping meetings minimal.

  6. Nº006

    How do you handle larger or complex projects?

    +

    Large initiatives are phased into clear stages: Research & Information Architecture, UX Wireframes & User Flows, High-Fidelity UI & Design Systems, and Validation & Developer Handoff. Each milestone follows a steady rhythm so you see tangible progress every week.

  7. Nº007

    Why work with an independent designer over an agency?

    +

    Traditional agencies add account managers, bureaucratic overhead, and inflated retainers. Working directly with me gives you direct, senior-level design firepower from day one, faster feedback loops, and solutions designed directly by the person doing the work.

  8. Nº008

    How do we share feedback and review iterations?

    +

    Primarily through contextual comments directly in Figma, supported by async Loom walkthroughs and structured Notion updates. If a brief or decision requires a live sync, we schedule a focused, agenda-driven call.

  9. Nº009

    What if a design needs revisions?

    +

    Design is inherently an iterative discipline. Every project milestone includes dedicated feedback rounds. I iterate collaboratively until the product achieves the right balance between user delight, business outcomes, and technical feasibility.

  10. Nº010

    Where are you based, and how do you handle time zones?

    +

    I am based in India (IST / UTC+5:30) and work smoothly with clients across the US, Europe, and Asia through disciplined asynchronous workflows, proactive updates, and flexible overlap hours for real-time syncs.

002 Scope of Work

  1. Nº001

    What types of design projects do you take on?

    +

    Web and mobile application design (iOS & Android), SaaS dashboard architecture, design systems and UI kits in Figma, interaction design and micro-animations, landing pages, and product strategy. If it lives on a screen and needs to be intuitive, I design it.

  2. Nº002

    What is outside of your typical scope?

    +

    Backend server development, complex 3D character animation, and legacy print production. For frontend development (Webflow, Framer, HTML/CSS/JS), I can build turnkey experiences directly or work hand-in-hand with your engineering team.

  3. Nº003

    Do you write microcopy and UX writing?

    +

    Yes. Interface copy—including button labels, empty states, onboarding tooltips, error messages, and micro-interactions—is designed as an integral part of the user experience to ensure clarity and reduce cognitive load.

  4. Nº004

    Can you code or develop the designs?

    +

    Yes. For websites and marketing experiences, I build responsive, production-ready sites in Webflow, Framer, and custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript. For product applications (React, Next.js, Flutter), I provide pixel-perfect Figma specs and work alongside engineers to ensure flawless implementation.

  5. Nº005

    Can you work within our existing brand guidelines and design system?

    +

    Absolutely. I can adapt seamlessly to your established brand guidelines and design token structure. If your existing design system needs refinement, auditing, or modernization, I can help scale it into a robust component library.

  6. Nº006

    Do you conduct user research and usability testing?

    +

    Yes. Research and testing are fundamental to my process. I conduct user interviews, competitive analysis, journey mapping, and interactive prototype testing to validate assumptions and eliminate usability friction before engineering starts.

  7. Nº007

    Can you design for multiple platforms?

    +

    Yes. I design responsive web applications and native mobile interfaces following Apple Human Interface Guidelines for iOS and Material Design standards for Android.

  8. Nº008

    Can we work on multiple product lines or brands?

    +

    Yes. Whether you are managing multiple sub-brands, cross-platform product suites, or different client accounts, we can coordinate all requests under a unified engagement structure.

003 Workflow & Collaboration

  1. Nº001

    How do you keep meetings to a minimum?

    +

    I prioritize focused deep work and asynchronous collaboration. Loom video walkthroughs, detailed Figma documentation, and structured Notion tasks replace 90% of recurring meetings. Live calls are reserved for high-leverage kickoffs and strategic alignment.

  2. Nº002

    What tools do you use in your daily workflow?

    +

    Figma is my primary workspace for interface design, design systems, and prototyping. Project tracking runs on Notion or Linear. For motion and animation, I work across After Effects and Jitter. Daily communication happens via Slack, Discord, or WhatsApp.

  3. Nº003

    How do you prepare handoff for developers?

    +

    Every Figma file is structured with clean auto-layout, named semantic tokens (typography, color palette, spacing scale), responsive variants, edge cases (empty states, errors, loading skeletons), and interactive clickable prototypes. Your developers get everything they need to build accurately.

  4. Nº004

    What do you need from me to get started?

    +

    A brief overview of your product, your target audience, core user problems, any existing brand or design assets, and timeline expectations. Even if your brief is just a few bullet points, we can refine it together during the kickoff.

  5. Nº005

    How quickly do you respond to messages?

    +

    Within a few hours on business days. For active projects, communication is prompt and transparent through your preferred messaging platform.

  6. Nº006

    Can my team join the project workspace?

    +

    Yes. Your engineers, product managers, founders, and stakeholders can all have full access to view files, review prototypes, and leave contextual feedback in Figma and Notion.

  7. Nº007

    What does a typical week of collaboration look like?

    +

    At the start of the week, we align on sprint priorities. Mid-week, I share work-in-progress drafts and interactive prototype links with Loom walkthroughs. By the end of the week, revised deliverables are finalized and staged for engineering review.

  8. Nº008

    What happens after a milestone is completed?

    +

    I conduct a thorough walkthrough of all final assets, ensure developers have all necessary tokens and assets, and package documentation. Once approved, we seamlessly transition into the next scheduled milestone.

004 Engagement & Pricing

  1. Nº001

    How do you structure pricing and engagements?

    +

    I work on two flexible models: fixed-scope project pricing (milestone-based for clearly defined scopes like MVPs, redesigns, or design systems) and dedicated monthly retainers (for teams requiring ongoing product design capacity). Pricing is transparent with zero hidden costs.

  2. Nº002

    What payment methods do you accept?

    +

    Bank wire transfers, Stripe, and international transfer platforms including Wise. Invoices are clearly itemized per milestone or billing cycle.

  3. Nº003

    Is there a long-term contract commitment?

    +

    No lock-in contracts. Project engagements are scoped strictly to agreed deliverables, and retainers operate on a flexible month-to-month basis that can be adjusted with reasonable notice.

  4. Nº004

    Can we start with a smaller pilot project?

    +

    Yes. Many teams prefer to begin with a design audit, a 1-week sprint, or a key feature redesign to experience the working rhythm before expanding into a full engagement.

  5. Nº005

    Can we pause or adjust our engagement?

    +

    Yes. If your engineering backlog is catching up or your roadmap shifts, we can pause or adjust retainer bandwidth so your budget aligns with your current development velocity.

  6. Nº006

    Are there any hidden setup or onboarding fees?

    +

    None. The agreed project scope or monthly retainer covers all design work, meetings, handoff, and documentation.

  7. Nº007

    How do you handle scope changes mid-project?

    +

    If your product requirements evolve during a sprint, we simply assess the changes together and either swap equivalent tasks or adjust the timeline and budget before proceeding.

  8. Nº008

    How do we get started?

    +

    Reach out via the contact form or email with a quick summary of what you're building. We'll set up a brief discovery call to discuss scope, timeline, and whether we're a great fit.

005 Files & Ownership

  1. Nº001

    Who owns the design work and intellectual property?

    +

    You do, 100%. Upon completion and final invoice payment, all design deliverables, components, assets, and intellectual property belong exclusively to your company.

  2. Nº002

    Will I receive all the source Figma files?

    +

    Yes. You receive complete edit access and full ownership of master Figma files, including organized component libraries, auto-layout screens, style guides, and design tokens.

  3. Nº003

    Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

    +

    Yes. If your product is unreleased or contains proprietary workflows, I am happy to sign your standard NDA before reviewing any confidential materials.

  4. Nº004

    Is my project kept confidential?

    +

    Confidential projects protected under an NDA are never displayed in public portfolios or case studies without your prior written authorization.

  5. Nº005

    What happens to our files if the engagement ends?

    +

    All files remain yours permanently. Master Figma files, prototype links, export packages, and documentation are organized and handed over in full.

  6. Nº006

    What file formats will I receive?

    +

    Native Figma project files, clickable interactive prototypes, vector SVG icons and illustrations, production-ready PNG/WebP exports, and design token documentation for your engineering team.
